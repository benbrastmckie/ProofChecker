SCOPE ENUMERATION -- deliverable 1 of the abstract-validity-layer refactor.
Measured against the working tree at the plan's HEAD, by a comment-aware declaration walker
(scratchpad/declscan.py) over the eight truth/validity modules under FormalSystem/Semantics/:
Truth, MinusTruth, PlusTruth, StarTruth, Validity, MinusValidity, PlusValidity, StarValidity.

MEASURED TOTAL: 242 declarations in the eight files.
(The broader territory -- Semantics/**, PlusLanguage/**, StarLanguage/**, MinusLanguage/** --
is larger; only these eight files carry the truth/validity layer this refactor abstracts.)

CATEGORIES
  A-def    validity-layer primitive `def`s: the four ValidOn/ValidOnFrames/ValidIn/Valid per
           language. Language-agnostic in SHAPE, but their bodies are deliberately NOT replaced
           (a `def` body replacement would change reducibility and `unfold`/`simp` behaviour at
           the ~787 `Valid` occurrences downstream). Covered by the class; not delegated.
  A-tagdef frame-class tag `def`s (ValidDense, ValidZTime, ...). Instances of ValidIn at a tag;
           untouched for the same reason.
  A-thm    validity-layer THEOREMS whose statements are identical modulo the formula type and
           the truth predicate. These are the bodies that get delegated to the generic layer.
  B        derived-operator clause lemmas, language-agnostic: provable from the primitive
           clauses alone, with no induction on the formula type.
  C        primitive clause lemmas: restatements of the truth recursion's own equations for the
           SHARED operators (bot, imp, box, untl, snce, stab; allFuture/allPast where primitive).
           These become the instance payload of the operator classes -- they are not delegated,
           they are what the instances supply.
  D        genuinely language-specific: everything proved by induction on the formula type
           (time-shift, congruence, state-locality, embedding bridges), everything about
           per-language constructors (atoms, timeStore/timeRecall), the consequence layer, the
           satisfiability layer, the frame-class bridge lemmas, and the correspondence
           structures.

CORRECTIONS TO THE RESEARCH REPORT'S SURVEY (report said 64/37/28/113):
  * A is 65, not 64. The report's tag-def count of 10 omitted `MinusValidZTimeSucc`
    (MinusValidity.lean:237), a fourth L-minus tag `def` at a frame class L has no counterpart
    for. 16 A-def + 11 A-tagdef + 38 A-thm = 65.
  * B is 39, not 37. The two extra are L-minus's `somePast_iff`/`someFuture_iff`, which the
    report held back as conditional on the tense-primitive `rfl` bridges. They are counted here
    as in-scope; whether they DELEGATE is settled by the bridge, and is recorded below.
  * C is 22, not 28. The report's 28 included six clause lemmas for constructors that are NOT
    shared across the languages and therefore cannot be instance payload of a shared operator
    class: `Truth.atom_iff_of_domain`, `Truth.atom_false_of_not_domain`, `PlusTruth.atom_iff`,
    `StarTruth.atom_iff`, `StarTruth.timeStore_iff`, `StarTruth.timeRecall_iff`. Those six are
    reclassified to D.
  * D is therefore 116, not 113 (65+39+22+116 = 242).

THE HONEST BOUNDARY (one criterion): the class abstracts the truth RELATION, not the inductive
TYPE, so it exposes no recursor. Anything proved by `induction phi` stays per-language. That is
what puts `timeShift_preserves_truth`, `plusTruthAt_timeShift`, `starTruthAt_timeShift`,
`truth_congr_ext`, `star_truth_congr_ext`, `stab_state_only`, `plusTruthAt_ofFormula`,
`starTruthAt_ofPlus`, `starValidOn_ofPlus`, `starValidOnFrames_ofPlus` and the atom/register
clause lemmas in D. None of these is a defect.
