### Incident: concurrent git-snapshot destroyed uncommitted work (recovered)

  Between the Phase 3 batch-3 edit and its commit, a concurrent dispatch in the same
  working tree ran `.claude/scripts/git-snapshot.sh`, which performs `git stash` +
  `git reset --hard HEAD` repo-wide. That swept 8 verified-green uncommitted edits in
  Soundness.lean into `stash@{0}` (git-snapshot-1788892063), together with the other
  dispatch's specs/state.json changes. `git reflog` records it as
  `HEAD@{0}: reset: moving to HEAD`.

  Recovery: the stash was NOT popped (it holds another dispatch's work, and popping it
  would inject that work into this task's tree). The 8 edits are mechanical and were
  re-applied by hand and committed immediately. Nothing was lost. Post-incident, every
  batch is committed as soon as its build goes green rather than at phase end.
