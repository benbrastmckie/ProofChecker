### Incident: concurrent git-snapshot destroyed uncommitted work (recovered)

  Between the Phase 3 batch-3 edit and its commit, a concurrent dispatch in the same
  working tree ran `.claude/scripts/git-snapshot.sh`, which performs `git stash` +
  `git reset --hard HEAD` repo-wide. That swept 8 verified-green uncommitted edits in
  Soundness.lean into `stash@{0}` (git-snapshot-1788892063), together with the other
  dispatch's specs/state.json changes. `git reflog` records it as
  `HEAD@{0}: reset: moving to HEAD`.

  Recovery: the stash was NOT popped (it holds another dispatch's work, and popping it
  would inject that work into this task's tree). The 8 edits are mechanical and were
  re-applied by hand and committed immediately. Nothing was lost. Post-incident, every
  batch is committed as soon as its build goes green rather than at phase end.

### The `swapTemporal`-through-connectives gap in `swap_norm` (follow-up candidate)

  Two independent lines of evidence, both observed here rather than assumed:

  1. At the eight FrameClassVariants `[Formula.swapTemporal, Formula.and, ...]` sites,
     NO list shorter than the original preserves the goal. Probed three candidates at
     site 292:
       `[Formula.swapTemporal, truth_norm]`     -> leaves `(p.and (φ.untl ψ)).swapTemporal`
                                                   unreduced (goal differs)
       `[swap_norm, Formula.and, truth_norm]`   -> leaves the OUTER `.swapTemporal`
       `[swap_norm, Formula.and, Formula.swapTemporal, truth_norm]`
                                                -> works, but is exactly as long as the
                                                   original: zero text reduction
  2. At Soundness `dense_indicator_swap_valid`, dropping `Formula.neg` in favour of
     `truth_norm` gives `simp made no progress` -- nothing in `truth_norm` matches a
     `.neg`-headed `untl` under `swapTemporal`.

  Diagnosis: `swap_norm`'s eleven lemmas push `swapTemporal` through the temporal and
  modal operators but NOT through `and`, `or`, or `imp`, so at every site whose formula
  has a propositional connective under the swap, the definitional `Formula.and` /
  `Formula.or` / `Formula.neg` unfolds remain load-bearing and cannot come out.

  Adding `swap_temporal_and`, `swap_temporal_or` and `swap_temporal_imp` to
  `FormalSystem/Syntax/Formula.lean` and tagging them `@[swap_norm]` would very likely
  unlock these nine sites. That is a change to a declaring module and therefore OUT OF
  SCOPE here (this task's Non-Goals forbid adding lemmas or `@[truth_norm]` tags), but
  it is a well-evidenced candidate for a follow-on task.
